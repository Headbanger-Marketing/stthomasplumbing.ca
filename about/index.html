Replacing a fixture or planning a bathroom installation?

For a toilet, faucet, tub, shower, or sink project in St. Thomas, the work depends on the fixture, existing connections, and whether the room is being renovated or newly finished. Photograph the current plumbing and measure the available space before comparing options. Unexpected water use, a damp patch, or a hidden leak?

A damp spot, unexplained water use, or a suspected hidden leak needs careful diagnosis before walls or floors are opened. Note when the symptom appears, photograph visible staining, and check whether it changes when fixtures are used. Depending on access and the suspected location, acoustic listening, thermal imaging, or camera inspection may help narrow the search. Ask what evidence supports the proposed opening or repair, especially for leaks beneath a slab or underground, where rerouting and direct repair have different scopes.
